Suggested research workflow

1. Search for studiesUse Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, ERIC, DOAJ, or other suitable databases. Search with focused keywords.
2. Read the original sourceCheck the abstract, method, participants, findings, limitations, and publication details.
3. Use AI to understand—not inventAsk ChatGPT, Anara, EchoGPT, or NotebookLM to explain a paper you provide. Verify every claim against the paper.
4. Organize referencesSave metadata and DOI/URL. Use Zotero or MyBib to help format citations, then check the required style.
5. Synthesize in your own wordsCompare studies by theme, method, findings, similarities, differences, and research gap. Do not copy AI output as your own work.
6. Back up your workExport or copy important notes and references to a separate document or cloud drive.

Privacy reminder: avoid uploading classmates' personal information, private school records, passwords, or other sensitive data to AI services.
